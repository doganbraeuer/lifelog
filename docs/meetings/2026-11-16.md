# Meeting on 2026-11-16

Prof. Pree, Dogan Bräuer, Dylan Müller, and a prospective first test user.
The app is to be used for real; a first test week follows. Next meeting in
about two weeks.

## Wishes

### Categories (main topic)

- Within health, categories with parent and child items, e.g. mood, sleep,
  muscle strength, medication, plus the document kinds (doctor's letter,
  lab report, ...). They replace the idea "forms with mandatory fields" of
  2026-11-09.
- The timeline over everything or filtered by one category.
- On upload a dialog: save to the timeline or not, with the categories as
  check boxes. The app ticks a suggestion, the person corrects it. One
  entry can have several categories. Buttons to upload or cancel.

### Routines (main topic)

- A routine for a period, e.g. measure blood pressure for two weeks, ice
  bathing twice a week, 30 g of protein a day.
- A reminder when it is forgotten; a button "neue Routine" at the bottom.
- Pause a routine, e.g. for a week.
- It should be motivating.

### Checking the values

- No check marks any more; mark in red only what does not match ("bitte
  prüfen"). If everything was found, simply say so.

### Voice notes

- Bug: long voice notes cannot be scrolled down.
- A type of its own for voice recordings, in a section like "Sonstiges".
- Title "Sprachnachricht vom ...", plus a short summary as title (e.g.
  "Schlaf").
- Edit the text of a saved voice note later.
- The check ("found in the original") looks different for voice notes: the
  original is the text itself.

### Statistics with AI, and questions

- Charts of values over time, e.g. blood pressure.
- Questions about the statistics: "When did my blood pressure get worse?",
  "Are blood pressure and sleep quality related?"
- Distinguish questions more precisely: "took vitamin C" is not
  "prescribed vitamin C".
- The model needs the series of numbers for that, not only text chunks.
  The rule "no medical judgement" stays: the app may show that two values
  rise together, not claim that one causes the other.

### Other

- Settings with a large icon.
- User management, encrypted data, secured database access.
- APK within a week, shared through a Google Drive link.
